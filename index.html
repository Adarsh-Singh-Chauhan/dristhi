A CLEAR PROCESS

From a forwarded claim to a reviewable case.

DRISHTI is designed to slow down risky sharing and help reviewers record what is known, what is missing and why a conclusion was reached.

01 · SUBMITCapture a claimPaste content you are authorised to review and add its public source URL.
02 · INSPECTSurface signalsExplainable rules flag selected urgency, forwarding, authority and link patterns.
03 · VERIFYCheck evidenceFind the primary source, date, location, original media and independent corroboration.
04 · CASE FILERecord a reviewOpen a structured case with claim details, evidence URLs and reviewer notes.
05 · DECIDEHuman conclusionSuperadmin records supported, false, misleading or inconclusive—only with evidence.
Important: the current analysis engine is a deterministic rule-based baseline. It does not independently fact-check the web, detect all deepfakes, or establish a claim as true or false. Reviewed conclusions require evidence and a human decision.

FROM SIGNAL TO EVIDENCE

Good tools don't jump to conclusions.

A message can be urgent and still be genuine. A quiet post can still be misleading. DRISHTI keeps the distinction visible: automated signals help prioritise attention; a reviewer documents evidence before assigning a case outcome.
