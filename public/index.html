Why an evidence-first workflow matters.

THE DRISHTI WORKFLOW

From a forwarded message
to a traceable case.

01

Submit

Paste a public claim or content you are authorised to review. Keep the original wording and source URL.

02

Inspect signals

Understand the matched language and link patterns, plus the limitations of the current baseline.

03

Preserve evidence

Create a case file with context, source references and a clear review request.

04

Human review

A reviewer records a sourced outcome—or leaves the matter open when evidence is insufficient.

MULTILINGUAL RESEARCH ROADMAP

Language-aware analysis.
Human-reviewed outcomes.

The product direction includes mBERT and XLM-RoBERTa research for English, Hindi and Hinglish, with evaluation by language and claim category. NetworkX graph signals are planned for coordinated public activity using lawful, privacy-minimised metadata. A Gemini-powered, cited-source threat Q&A assistant is a future integration and is not connected today.

These model and graph components are not active in the production analyser yet. A verified, licensed dataset and independent evaluation are required before a model can influence live review.

What DRISHTI does today
  • Deterministic, explainable text-pattern signals
  • Evidence-linked case creation and review workflow
  • Human-reviewed case outcomes
What it does not claim
  • No live social-media crawler or bot verdict
  • No trained misinformation model deployed
  • No automatic truth / false verdict or takedown
