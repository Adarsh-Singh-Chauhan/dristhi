    TRANSPARENCY BY DESIGN

    Understand the signals.

    DRISHTI's current baseline is intentionally simple and explainable. Learn what it can—and cannot—tell you.

    ↗

    Urgency & sharing pressure

    Looks for language that pressures readers to act immediately or forward a message widely.

    Surface-level language pattern
    ⌁

    Authority & forwarding claims

    Highlights authority references without an in-text source and markers that suggest the original context may be missing.

    Source context is not verified
    !

    Panic & formatting signals

    Surfaces alarm-oriented wording, unusually high capitalisation and repeated emphatic punctuation.

    Not evidence of falsity
    ◎

    Link review

    Recognises selected common URL shorteners so readers know to inspect the destination safely.

    No malware scan is performed
    ⓘ
    What the score means

    The score is a triage aid based on matched rules. It is not a probability, credibility rating, fact-check result or judgement about a person, party or ideology. New models must be evaluated for false positives and bias before use.
